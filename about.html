About Funstars

Practical digital tools for international education

We are an education technology company focused on building practical digital tools for international education. Our goal is to make academic practice, assessment, and learning progress easier to manage through technology.

Who we are

We currently focus on major international education programs and examinations, including SAT, ACT, AP, A Level, IGCSE, and HKDSE.

We continue to develop learning technology that can serve individual students, teachers, and education organizations across international markets.

Our platforms help students, teachers, and education providers manage practice, testing, learning progress, and academic performance more efficiently.

How Our Business Works

We develop education software and digital learning platforms for students and education providers.

  • Development of online education software
  • Operation of digital learning platforms
  • Development and management of academic question banks
  • Online assessment and testing technology
  • Subscription-based digital learning services
  • Technology solutions for education providers

Depending on the product, users may access free learning tools as well as paid premium features and services.

Who We Serve

Students

Students can practice questions, review mistakes, monitor progress, and prepare for examinations through structured digital learning tools.

Teachers

Teachers can assign learning activities, review student performance, and identify areas that require additional support.

Education Providers

Schools, tutoring organizations, and education service providers can use digital learning and assessment tools to support their students and teaching operations.
