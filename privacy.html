Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Funstars LLC (“Funstars”, “we”, “us”) provides digital learning platforms and this website. This policy explains how we handle information collected through our public website and contact forms.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, organization, role, and the contents of your message. We may also collect standard technical information such as browser type, device type, and pages visited when you use this website.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries from students, teachers, schools, education providers, and partners
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website and products
  • To communicate about our platforms and services when you have requested information
  • To meet legal, security, and operational requirements

Education and student data

Our learning platforms may process academic activity such as practice results, progress, and performance analytics. Product-specific terms and privacy notices apply when an institution or individual uses a Funstars learning platform. We design our systems so that student, teacher, and institution data can be managed according to the relevant product configuration.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our website and platforms, or when required by law.

Retention

We retain inquiry and operational information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Funstars LLC at contact@funstarslearning.com.
